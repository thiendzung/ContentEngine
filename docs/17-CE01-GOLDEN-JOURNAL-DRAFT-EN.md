# How to understand the price of an original painting: a first-time buyer’s guide

An artwork price is information, not a score. There is no single fixed formula that explains every original painting. Specialists may look at several concrete facts around a work, while your final decision still has a personal part.

You do not need to understand the whole art market before looking at a painting. Start smaller: understand what is known about the work, what the price can help you place in context, and what the number cannot decide for you.

## What a price can tell you — and what it cannot

| Read the price alongside | Do not use the price to decide |
|---|---|
| who made the work | whether you are “good at art” |
| material and dimensions | whether you should love the work |
| documented ownership history, when it exists | whether a more expensive work is automatically the right work for you |
| condition and relevant market context | whether you want to live with it |
| practical costs of bringing the work home | your personal taste |

A useful price conversation should make the work clearer, not make the buyer feel smaller.

## What can affect the price of an original painting?

There is no universal equation. In professional assessment, several factors may be considered together.

Examples include the artist or maker, the work’s origin and documented history, date, materials, dimensions, rarity or type, subject, condition and comparable sales. Different works can give different weight to those factors, so a list like this is context, not a calculator.

One term that can sound more complicated than it is is **provenance**. It simply means the documented ownership or collecting history of a work. When that history is clear, it can help establish what is known about the work. When it is not documented, it should not be replaced with a confident story.

For this guide, that distinction matters more than memorising art-market vocabulary: ask what is actually known, and separate it from what is assumed.

## Does a higher price mean a better painting?

A higher price should not be a shortcut for your own judgement.

At MOTGU, we treat price as one piece of information around a work, not as a score for taste. You can understand the facts, look at the painting again, and still decide that another work matters more to you.

Take your time. A first purchase does not need to prove that you understand art. It needs to be a work you understand well enough to choose without being pushed.

## What else should you check before taking a painting home?

The artwork price may not be the whole practical cost of bringing a work home.

MOTGU’s product rules allow shipping costs to include packaging, shipping and insurance. An oversize work may need a separate quote. The exact amount and process depend on the specific work and destination, so they should be checked when you are considering that work.

If you are visiting Hanoi and thinking about buying a painting, it helps to separate two questions:

1. **What is the current price of the work?**
2. **What would bringing this specific work home involve?**

That second question is practical, not glamorous — and often more useful than another paragraph about the art market.

## Five questions to ask before you decide

### 1. What exactly do we know about this work?

Start with the basics: artist, year, material, dimensions and any documented history or work information that is actually available.

### 2. Which documented facts help me understand its context?

Ask which facts are known and which are interpretation. Do not turn a list of factors into a formula for the “correct” price.

### 3. Is there provenance, condition information or other history that is actually documented?

If documentation exists, ask what it says. If it does not, an absence should remain an absence rather than becoming a sales story.

### 4. What would bringing this specific work home involve?

Ask about packaging, shipping, insurance and whether the work needs a separate quote. Keep those practical costs distinct from the artwork price itself.

### 5. After I understand the facts, do I still want to live with it?

This is the part nobody else can answer for you.

The facts can make the decision clearer. They cannot make the personal decision disappear.
