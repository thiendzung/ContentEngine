# CE02 closeout

## Proven

- Structured persistence now traces a content record from Signal and human selection through ContentCase, locale-specific variants, evidence, settings, run, artifacts, approval and ContentVersion.
- SettingsSnapshot, locked EvidenceSet, ContextManifest, Artifact and ContentVersion are protected from overwrite where reproducibility requires it.
- English and Vietnamese LocaleVariant records are independent while sharing one ContentCase.
- Research and Opportunity Map outputs use versioned Artifact records with locale, source and provenance references.
- Media observations remain candidate data until explicitly approved; model observations are not canonical facts.

## Debt and CE03 boundary

- CE03 owns durable queueing, workers, lease/heartbeat, bounded retries, resume, outbox and reconciliation.
- No research automation, model routing, tool adapters, UI/dashboard, vector search or WordPress publishing was added.

## Lessons

- Database triggers are required for immutable audit artifacts; application conventions alone do not protect history.
- Version lineage is clearest when a ContentVersion points directly to its creating run and final Artifact.

## CE03 starting point

CE03 starts from the persisted ContentRun, StepRun, Artifact, Approval, ContextManifest, ModelCall and ToolCall ledger created in CE02.
