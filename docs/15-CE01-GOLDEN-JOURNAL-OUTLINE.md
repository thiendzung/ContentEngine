## 1. V2 PRINCIPLE — HUMAN FIRST, MACHINE LEGIBLE

The page must work for a reader who:

- arrives from Google, Bing, an AI answer, a recommendation or an internal MOTGU link;
- may scan first on mobile before deciding whether to read deeply;
- may know little about buying original art;
- wants a useful answer quickly, not an art-market lecture;
- may later ask an AI system a more specific follow-up question;
- may be in Hanoi and deciding whether to inspect or buy a real work.

The page must therefore:

1. answer the main question immediately;
2. use clear question-led headings where they naturally match reader intent;
3. keep each section focused on one job;
4. distinguish verified facts from MOTGU guidance and personal judgement;
5. use one real MOTGU Artwork as first-party evidence/context;
6. provide useful images of that real Artwork when available;
7. make related Artwork / Artist / Visit paths crawlable and useful;
8. avoid repeating query variants just to capture keywords or AI fan-out queries.

### Reverse-thinking guard

Do **not** optimize by:

- making the article longer for its own sake;
- adding many tiny sections purely for AI extraction;
- repeating `price of art`, `art prices`, `how is art priced` unnaturally;
- creating a fake FAQ section only for search appearance;
- turning every nearby query into a separate thin article;
- using an `llms.txt` or special AI markup as a substitute for useful content;
- adding unsupported numbers, formulas, market claims or “expert” language;
- writing a generic “7 factors that determine art price” article with MOTGU added at the end.

The article should contain information that is difficult to reproduce without MOTGU's real Artwork, first-party product facts, practical buying context and editorial position.

# QUICK DECISION MAP — ABOVE THE DEEP EXPLANATION

## Working heading

**What a price can tell you — and what it cannot**

### Purpose

Give scanners a useful mental model in seconds and create a compact, verifiable summary that search/AI systems can understand without losing nuance.

### Recommended visible format

A simple two-column table or paired list.

**A price can be understood alongside:**

- facts about the work and maker;
- materials and dimensions;
- documented history/provenance when present;
- condition and comparable market context where relevant;
- practical costs around taking the work home.

**A price cannot decide:**

- whether the work is “better” than another work;
- whether the buyer should love it;
- whether it will rise in value;
- whether it is the right first purchase for this person.

# SECTION 1 — WHAT CAN AFFECT THE PRICE OF AN ORIGINAL PAINTING?

## Working heading

**What can affect the price of an original painting?**

### Purpose

Answer the largest nearby factual follow-up query while keeping the art-market explanation bounded.

### Answer-first sentence contract

Start with one concise sentence saying specialists may consider several factors and there is no universal equation.

Then explain only the factors the current EvidenceSet supports, such as:

- artist/maker;
- provenance/ownership history when documented;
- materials;
- dimensions;
- rarity/type/subject where relevant;
- condition;
- comparable sales in specialist assessment.

### Plain-language requirement

If `provenance` appears, define it immediately in ordinary language, for example as the documented ownership/history of a work.

Do not assume collector vocabulary.

### Evidence / input refs

- `CLAIM-02` → `SRC-02`;
- `CLAIM-03` → `SRC-03`;
- `CLAIM-01` for the non-formula guard.

### Writer guard

Frame these as **examples of factors specialists may consider**.

Do not imply:

- every factor applies equally to every work;
- one factor mechanically determines price;
- size × time × reputation is a valid formula;
- a specific MOTGU price was calculated from these factors;
- this section is an exhaustive valuation guide.

### Length guard

Keep this section shorter than the real-MOTGU-work + practical sections combined.

The article's value must not depend on repeating generic market knowledge.

### Reader movement

`usable mental model → enough factual context`

# SECTION 2 — DOES A HIGHER PRICE MEAN A BETTER PAINTING?

## Working heading

**Does a higher price mean a better painting?**

### Purpose

Address the hidden fear behind the price question: the buyer may be treating the number as a judgement of artistic quality or of their own taste.

### Answer direction

No. The article can explain factual context around the price, but the number cannot make the personal decision for the buyer.

### Evidence / input refs

- ContentCase reader-before/reader-after;
- selected Angle A;
- POS-01, POS-04;
- OriginalityPack `ORIG-04`.

### Claim type

Primarily editorial guidance / interpretation, not a factual market-ranking claim.

### Writer guard

# V2 SEARCH / AI DISCOVERY RATIONALE

This revision deliberately follows current platform guidance without treating SEO/AEO/AIO/GEO as four separate writing styles.

Applied principles:

1. **Non-commodity first-party value** — one real MOTGU Artwork becomes the centre, because generic summaries are easy to reproduce.
2. **Answer early** — primary question receives a direct bounded answer before deeper explanation.
3. **Clear structure** — descriptive headings and focused sections help both scanning humans and retrieval systems.
4. **Natural adjacent-question coverage** — nearby questions are covered on one coherent page rather than through thin fan-out pages.
5. **Evidence clarity** — factual claims stay traceable to locked sources; editorial guidance is labeled by function.
6. **Multimodal value** — real Artwork imagery supports the text rather than decorating it.
7. **Entity clarity** — Artwork, Artist, MOTGU and Hanoi are explicit when relevant.
8. **No AI hacks** — no special AI markup, forced chunking, keyword repetition or fake FAQ expansion.
9. **Useful next action** — internal links lead to the real Artwork/Artist/Visit path that continues the reader's job.
10. **Dynamic facts stay dynamic** — price/availability are verified live instead of copied from memory.
