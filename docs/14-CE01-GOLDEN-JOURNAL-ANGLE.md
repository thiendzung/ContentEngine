# ANGLE A — READ THE PRICE WITHOUT TREATING IT AS A SCORE

**Recommended**

### Working title direction

> **How to understand the price of an original painting: a first-time buyer’s guide**

### Core idea

A price is useful information, but it is not a score for how “good” a painting is and it is not a command to buy. Help the reader separate three things:

1. concrete factors specialists may consider when assessing a work;
2. facts about the specific physical work in front of them;
3. the personal decision of whether they want to live with that work.

### Reader promise

> You will leave knowing what a displayed price can tell you, what it cannot tell you, and which questions to ask before deciding.

### Reader transformation

Before:

> “I like it, but I do not understand the number and I am afraid of making a foolish first purchase.”

After:

> “I can separate market/context facts from my own preference, ask better questions, and decide without pretending to be an expert.”

### Emotional direction

`uncertainty → orientation → calm confidence`

No status anxiety. No collector performance. No pressure.

### Originality

MOTGU does not try to teach the entire art market. It brings the discussion back to the real physical artwork and the practical buying moment:

- live work facts;
- current price/availability from canonical data;
- size/material/status;
- practical shipping/delivery context;
- calm artist-house guidance.

### Evidence coverage

Strong enough with `es_ce01_price_v1`:

- no single universal fixed formula;
- examples of factors specialists consider;
- bounded provenance explanation;
- MOTGU canonical product/commerce facts;
- practical separation between artwork price and taking-it-home costs.

### MOTGU connection

Natural path:

`Journal → specific Artwork → Ask anything / Visit / Inquiry`

The Artwork remains the centre of gravity.

### Risks

- could become a generic valuation explainer if it spends too long listing factors;
- must not imply every factor determines a MOTGU price;
- must not imply price predicts quality, future value or investment return;
- current price/availability cannot be written from memory.

### Guard

Do not cross into:

> “How MOTGU calculates its prices.”

That requires new founder/artist evidence.

# ANGLE B — WHAT ARE YOU ACTUALLY PAYING FOR?

### Working title direction

> **What are you actually paying for when you buy an original painting?**

### Core idea

Start from the buyer’s natural question: “What is behind this number?” Explain the difference between the work’s observable facts, market/context factors, practical take-home costs and the part that remains personal judgement.

### Reader promise

> You will understand the different layers around an artwork price so the number feels less opaque.

### Reader transformation

`opaque number → understandable layers → better questions`

### Emotional direction

`confusion → curiosity → clarity`

### Originality

Strong opportunity to use MOTGU physical-work and shipping context rather than abstract art-market theory.

### Evidence coverage

Mostly covered by the locked EvidenceSet.

### MOTGU connection

Very natural connection to a real Artwork and the practical question of bringing it home.

### Risks

**Higher risk than Angle A.** The title can sound like MOTGU is about to explain exactly how a price is constructed. Current EvidenceSet does not contain MOTGU/artist base-pricing methodology.

To stay valid, the article would need repeated qualification and could become awkward.

### Decision note

Usable, but not preferred for CE01 because the title creates a stronger factual promise than the locked evidence comfortably supports.

# ANGLE C — SIX QUESTIONS BEFORE YOUR FIRST ORIGINAL ART PURCHASE

### Working title direction

> **Six questions to ask before buying your first original painting**

### Core idea

Turn the evidence into a simple practical checklist:

- What exactly is the work?
- Who made it?
- What do we know about material, size, condition/history?
- What does the displayed price include?
- What will bringing it home involve?
- Do I still want to live with it after I understand the facts?

### Reader promise

> You do not need to become an art-market expert. You need a small set of useful questions for the work in front of you.

### Reader transformation

`intimidated beginner → prepared visitor/buyer`

### Emotional direction

`anxiety → practical control → personal confidence`

### Originality

Very strong fit with MOTGU’s low-pressure artist-house posture and real-work context.

### Evidence coverage

Good for the checklist, provided condition/provenance wording remains bounded and live commerce facts are read at drafting time.

### MOTGU connection

Excellent natural CTA: see a work, ask about it, take time, understand what bringing it home involves.

### Risks

- drifts away from the selected `price` opportunity into a broader first-purchase guide;
- could compete with a future broader “first art purchase” Pillar;
- weaker match to the verified `price` cluster.

### Decision note

Good future cluster/pillar input, but not the best first CE01 test for the selected `price` opportunity.
